## Notes for deciding

- **The 1976 film has two rights statements that do not match.** The Internet Archive item, uploaded in 2016 by a community account, sets CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The film's own end card (source 14:30) reads "Copyright © Brown University 1976" and shows no Creative Commons notice. If the license matters to you, ask Brown University (or the Brown CS department) to confirm it.
- **NC in CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 forbids commercial use.** Whether a launch film counts as commercial is your call. **SA (share-alike)** means an adapted version must be shared under the same license. Whether trimming, recoloring or cutting the clip into the film counts as adapting it was not checked here.
- **CC BY-SA images**: credit the author and license on screen or in the end credits. The same share-alike question applies if you crop or tint them.
- The on-screen attribution lines for Commons files follow the "Author, License, via Wikimedia Commons" format of the Commons "Use this file" dialog. They were built from each page's Author and License fields, not copied from the dialog.
- The Xanadu Space clip is a YouTube video by a third party, mirrored on the Internet Archive. Nobody has granted a license for it.
